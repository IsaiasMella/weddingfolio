Destination Weddings: How We Plan Coverage

January 18, 2026·Elena Moreau

Destination weddings ask for more than a plane ticket. They ask for pacing, local knowledge, and a photographer who can adapt.

Arrival matters

Covering a welcome dinner or rehearsal often becomes some of the most cherished coverage — quieter, warmer, less rushed than the wedding day itself.

Scouting is part of the craft

We arrive early when possible to walk light, find portraits that feel intentional, and learn the venue’s rhythm.

Weather is a collaborator

Rain, wind, and harsh midday sun are not failures. They are invitations to shoot differently — covered walkways, candlelit rooms, stormy skies.

Delivery timelines

Travel and peak season can extend editing by a short window. Your contract always states the expected delivery so there are no surprises.
