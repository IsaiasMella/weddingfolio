FAQ

Answers before you inquire.

Do you travel for weddings?+

Yes. Destination weddings are a favorite. Travel fees depend on location — inquire with your date and venue for a clear quote.

How far in advance should we book?+

Peak season dates often book 9–14 months ahead. If your date is soon, reach out anyway — last-minute openings happen.

When will we receive our photos?+

Signature Day galleries are typically delivered within 6–8 weeks. Destination and peak-season timelines are confirmed in your contract.

Do you offer albums or prints?+

Yes. Heirloom albums and fine-art prints can be added after your gallery is live. We partner with trusted labs for archival quality.

Can we request a second photographer?+

Signature Day includes a second photographer. For Elopement packages, a second shooter can be added when available.
