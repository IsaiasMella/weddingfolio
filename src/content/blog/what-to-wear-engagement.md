Engagement sessions should feel like a date with better light — not a costume change.

## Choose movement over formality

If you cannot walk, sit, or laugh easily in it, leave it at home. Soft fabrics photograph beautifully and keep you relaxed.

## Coordinate, do not match

Think complementary tones: ivory with charcoal, sage with warm neutrals, deep navy with soft blush. Matching outfits often flatten the frame.

## Bring one change if you want range

A casual look and a slightly dressier option give variety without turning the session into a fashion marathon.

## Trust comfort first

The best portraits happen when you forget the camera. Wear the shoes you can actually stand in for an hour.
