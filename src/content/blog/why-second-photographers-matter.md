A second photographer is not a luxury add-on for large weddings alone. It is insurance for emotion.

## Two perspectives at once

While one of us documents your walk down the aisle, the other catches parents’ faces. Both moments deserve a frame.

## Details without delay

Getting-ready rooms move quickly. A second shooter can gather flat lays and room atmosphere while portraits begin.

## Coverage when rooms split

First looks, cocktail hour, and reception setups often happen in parallel. Two photographers keep the story complete.

## A calmer experience for you

You feel less pulled. We move efficiently. Your memories stay whole.
