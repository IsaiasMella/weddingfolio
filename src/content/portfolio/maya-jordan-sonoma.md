Maya and Jordan wanted a day that felt like home — even though half the guest list flew in. Their Sonoma garden venue delivered oaks, soft wind, and a golden hour that cooperated perfectly.

We began with quiet getting-ready portraits, moved into a ceremony under the trees, and closed with dancing under string lights long after the last toast.
